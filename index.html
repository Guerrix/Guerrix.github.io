About

Over the years, I've found that the hardest engineering problems rarely come down to writing code. They usually involve understanding existing systems, navigating constraints, choosing trade-offs, and getting reliable software into production.

My experience spans fintech, consumer products, healthcare and wellness platforms, startups, and consulting engagements, mostly around mobile architecture and the systems behind it. More recently, that includes how AI-assisted development changes, and doesn't change, that work.

I'm based in Hermosillo, Sonora, Mexico, and work with distributed teams remotely.

Selected Work

Engineering problems I've owned, and the judgment required to solve them.

01

Security · System Design · Cross-Functional Delivery

Security and account protection in a regulated fintech platform

In a regulated fintech environment, account protection is a systems problem: identity verification, authentication, fraud signals, and operational constraints all have to hold together in production. I owned the architecture, implementation, and rollout of a KYC Refresh initiative, coordinating across backend, compliance, product, security, and mobile teams. That work sat alongside broader security initiatives (OTP, 2FA, Secure Code, Trusted Contacts), hardening from penetration-testing findings such as jailbreak detection and Face ID vulnerability mitigation, and evaluating third-party fraud and security vendors where they fit the threat model.

02

Release Engineering · Reliability · Technical Leadership

Owning mobile releases beyond "code complete"

Shipping mobile software is rarely finished when the implementation is. As iOS Release Manager during high-impact release cycles, I coordinated release readiness, stability work, and production-issue mitigation across cross-functional teams. The judgment call was usually not "is the feature built?" but whether the build, pipelines, and surrounding teams were actually ready for production, drawing on experience with CI/CD and production mobile releases.

03

Architecture · Performance · Media

Modernizing a media-heavy mobile platform

As Lead iOS Engineer on a high-engagement consumer video platform, the work was less a single migration than evolving multiple layers of a production system at once. That included contributing to an OpenGL-to-Metal rendering migration, building a custom video caching module to improve playback and loading reliability, integrating Apple Vision improvements for face detection and personalization, and establishing CircleCI and Fastlane release pipelines, while participating in technical direction, planning, estimation, and prioritization.
