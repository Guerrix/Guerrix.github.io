About Me

I'm a Senior iOS Engineer with 15+ years of experience building and scaling mobile products across fintech, healthcare, consumer, and startup environments.

Throughout my career, I've helped teams launch products from scratch, modernize legacy applications, improve engineering practices, and deliver mobile platforms used by thousands of users.

My expertise spans mobile architecture, security, performance optimization, release management, and cross-functional collaboration. I enjoy solving complex product and technical challenges while balancing scalability, maintainability, and delivery speed.

Most recently at Bitso, I worked on security-critical initiatives including KYC refresh flows, authentication systems (OTP and 2FA), anti-phishing protections, trusted contacts, and other account security features within a regulated fintech environment.

Previously, as Lead iOS Engineer at JibJab, I helped drive architectural decisions, led the migration of the rendering pipeline from OpenGL to Metal, and improved performance across a multimedia-heavy consumer application.

In addition to full-time roles, I've partnered with startups and enterprise clients through consulting engagements, helping teams ship products, improve engineering workflows, scale mobile architectures, and navigate critical technical decisions.

I’m passionate about building reliable software, mentoring engineers, and sharing practical lessons from real-world product development.

I’m based in Hermosillo, Sonora, Mexico I'm available for remote opportunities, consulting engagements, and technical leadership roles.
